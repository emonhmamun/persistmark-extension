    Privacy

    PersistMark stores everything locally in your browser (chrome.storage). It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no network requests — the only outbound data is an optional Chrome Sync upload controlled by the toggle above. The <all_urls> permission is required only to inject the highlighter into the pages you visit.

    Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+H toggle highlighter mode · Alt+Shift+L highlight selection · Alt+Shift+D remove highlights in selection. (Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts)
